## How they evaluate

Thirty seconds, skeptical (brief-a Visitor). First question is what kind of thing this is: a category, a QMS, a workflow tool, or an integration layer (11_5). They read the architecture, not the feature list, and note whether the vendor owns records or only shuffles them. Then they check the enterprise basics. What they forward to a direct report is a one-line description of where it fits. What kills it is a page that reads as a generic workflow product with vision language on top.
