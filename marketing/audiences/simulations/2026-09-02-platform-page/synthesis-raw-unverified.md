- **Same resonance score, different willingness to escalate.** ehs-process-owner, ops-director-regulated-mfg, and regulatory-affairs-lead all score 2 and choose nextAction "talk-to-sales," while vp-quality-med-device also scores 2 but chooses the more cautious "keep-reading." vp-quality-med-device's own confidence note: "the reaction to the QMS-replacement gap and the duplicated testimonials extends the persona file's stated objections." ## Verdict

**Decision:** what the rebuilt platform page should keep, cut, or reframe from the current live page, per audience segment.

**Keep**, for the regulated-workflow segments (Quality, Regulatory, Operations, EH&S): the named, specific feature vocabulary that is already landing, Real-Time Audit Trail, CFR Part 11 Compliant eSignatures, Revision Management / Version Control, Cycle Time Tracking, No-Code Process Builder, and Reminder & Escalation Workflows. These are the only elements any persona cited as positives, and they are cited by two or three personas each (see "What worked").

**Cut, for every segment, no split:** the sevenfold repeated company boilerplate paragraph and the duplicated testimonial quotes (3 of 4 cards share identical text). This is the only objection with unanimous or near-unanimous reach (4 personas objecting outright, 2 more noticing it) and no persona defends it. Per vp-quality-med-device: "duplicated customer evidence undercuts its own pitch." Fixing this is the single highest-leverage, lowest-risk change available; it is copy hygiene, not repositioning.

**Reframe, per segment:**
- *Finance (cfo-regulated-mfg):* the page currently has no path to resonance above 1 for this segment. Reframe the outcome language from generic ("measurable outcomes," feature lists) to one credible, real, non-duplicated cost or payback figure tied to a cycle-time claim, or accept that Finance will not be moved by this page and route them elsewhere in the funnel.
- *CIO / cold category researcher (cio-category-researcher):* state a single, explicit category claim once, in the hero or H1, in Unifize's own words ("system of record" and whatever the coordination-tax positioning settles on), rather than only inside the repeated boilerplate. Without this, the segment the homepage brief names as its cold-visitor target is predicted to bounce.
- *Non-QMS / EH&S (ehs-process-owner):* add at least one testimonial or feature line naming a non-quality workflow (incident, permit, corrective action). The page currently escalates this persona to "talk-to-sales" on generic language alone; a first-look example is a lower-cost way of moving comprehension from inferred to confirmed and reducing the chance sales loses them on the qualifying call.
- *Quality (vp-quality-med-device) and Regulatory (regulatory-affairs-lead):* name CAPA, deviation, and change order explicitly instead of only "records," state plainly whether Unifize replaces or sits beside a QMS, and add one substantiation point for CFR Part 11 / SOC-2 claims (an audit trail export example, a validation or CSV statement, or a sentence on chat-to-record immutability). These two segments carry the most first-look trust and the most first-look scrutiny simultaneously.
- *Operations (ops-director-regulated-mfg):* name Outlook and Teams alongside or instead of leading with Slack, and attach one real before/after cycle-time number or ECO example higher on the page. This segment explicitly says it has "nothing concrete to forward to the COO yet."

**Confidence: medium.** The four regulated-workflow personas converge tightly and independently on the same page elements (audit trail and eSignature language as positive, duplicated testimonials and buried category claim as negative), which is a stronger signal than any single persona's read. But per every persona's own confidence note, the exact threshold that would move a given segment up one resonance level (how big a number, how much substantiation) is inferred from the persona's stated evaluation habits, not observed against this specific rendering. This is a synthetic panel; treat the direction as reliable and the magnitude as unproven.

**Falsifiable predictions (calibration hooks):**
- If the rebuilt page removes the duplicated testimonial quotes and the sevenfold repeated boilerplate, then Finance, Regulatory, Operations, and Quality-segment readers will stop citing "template" or "copy paste" style credibility objections in qualitative feedback or first-call notes.
- If the rebuilt page states a single explicit category claim in the hero or H1 instead of only generic vision language, then CIO / cold category-research visitors will show a lower bounce rate on this page than the current baseline.
- If the rebuilt page adds a real dollar figure or payback period tied to the cycle-time claim, then Finance-segment visitors will move past an initial "bounce" reaction toward at least reading further or requesting more detail.
- If the rebuilt page adds one named non-quality workflow example (incident, permit, or corrective action outside quality), then EH&S / non-QMS process-owner visitors will report higher confidence that their workflows fit, not just that the tooling is generically appealing.
- If the rebuilt page names CAPA, deviation, and change order explicitly and states plainly whether Unifize replaces or sits beside a QMS, then Quality-segment visitors will be more willing to escalate to a sales conversation than the current "keep-reading" holding pattern.
- If the rebuilt page adds one substantiation point for its compliance badges (an audit trail export example, a validation or CSV statement, or a chat-to-record immutability statement), then Regulatory-segment visitors will treat the CFR Part 11 and SOC-2 claims as evidence rather than as labels.
- If the rebuilt page names Outlook and Teams explicitly and adds a real before/after cycle-time number tied to an example change order, then Operations-segment visitors will report having something concrete to escalate internally, rather than "nothing concrete to forward to the COO."

## Stakes flags

- **Pricing/business-case claims (validate-with-real):** any dollar figure, payback period, or ROI calculator added to satisfy the Finance objection must use real, verifiable customer data. The panel's own objection is that the existing "30% faster" claim already loses credibility once duplicated testimonials are noticed; inventing or rounding a number to fill this gap would compound the exact credibility problem being fixed.
- **Compliance and regulatory claims (validate-with-real):** CFR Part 11 and SOC-2 are real, legally meaningful compliance regimes. Any added substantiation (audit trail export examples, validation or CSV documentation statements, claims about chat-to-record immutability) must be reviewed by whoever owns Unifize's actual compliance posture before publishing; a synthetic panel can flag that proof is missing, but cannot validate that a specific added claim is true.
- **Customer testimonials and quotes (validate-with-real):** the recommendation to fix or replace the duplicated testimonial quotes touches real customer names and attributions (Wilson Lin, Denis Machoka, Jesse Kolstad, Clarissa Archer per the stimulus). Any replacement quotes or corrected attributions need real customer sign-off, not synthesized copy.
- **Category repositioning (validate-with-real):** the recommendation to state a "system of record" or coordination-tax category claim once, explicitly, in the hero is a repositioning decision tied to the broader site rebuild strategy. This panel is directional signal that the current burial of that claim is a problem; it is not sufficient basis on its own to finalize new positioning language without real-visitor testing.
