THE STAIN (hero)

Floor damage · cream-brown gradient · ~7 overlapping blobs

~22 residue fragments — quiet, mostly blank

The pile is dwindling but the floor remembers.

Design intent: the gap is permanent damage. Even if the residue were cleared away tomorrow, the floor would still be marked. That's the structural point of the doc — the gap is architectural, not behavioural; the cost has been accumulating in the seam for so long it's stained the substrate. The two clusters are deliberately backgrounded so the eye lands in the middle.
