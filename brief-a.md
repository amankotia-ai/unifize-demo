## Visitor

A cold prospect or a CIO/CEO doing category research. Has not heard "coordination tax" before. Arrived via thought leadership, organic search, or a peer referral. Mental state: curious but skeptical, scanning for whether this is a real category or another workflow tool. Will give the page about thirty seconds.

This approach also serves Visitor A from the Website Rebuild Brief (cold prospect anchored on the Two Systems and Hidden Operating Model sections).

## Load-bearing bet

The buyer does not know coordination tax exists. They must feel the gap and see its cost before they will accept that there is a layer that removes it. The diagnosis comes first because the diagnosis is the differentiator.

## Three takeaways, in scroll order

1. **Above the fold.** "I recognize this. My week looks like this."
2. **Second.** "Five days of work. Ninety-two days of calendar. The gap is not effort, it is waiting."
3. **Third.** "There is a name for that gap and there is a layer that removes it."

## Hero visual

**It is.** An abstract chaos collage. Three rotated frames: a calendar with no labels on the events, an inbox with no readable subjects, a shared drive with generic file names ("Investigation_v3_FINAL_v2", "Meeting_notes"). Cycling pain rotator beneath the headline ("Investigation still open from last quarter", "Approval stuck across four inboxes"). No CTA larger than a button.

**It is not.** Specific record types written on the screen (CAPA, ECO, deviation, SCAR, recall, 510(k), QMS). Per Ben in the call: "you can't be a QMS company on the homepage if you're trying to do this." It is also not the product. The product earns its appearance in section 6 onwards, not in the hero.

## CTAs

- **Hero.** Book a demo (primary, Unifize blue). See it live (ghost, links to the live thread embed).
- **Bottom.** Book a demo (primary). Calculate your coordination tax (ghost, seeds the engine page).

## Where this fails

The product takes too long to appear. A warm ICP who arrived from outbound and just wants to see the thing may bounce before section 6. Per Lakshman's call estimate that 95% of visits are link-driven, this approach optimises for the 5% cold case at the cost of the 95% warm case. The compensating control is: warm ICPs from outbound usually arrive on industry or domain pages, not the homepage, so the homepage can afford to serve cold visitors first.

## Adherence to locked rules

- Tagline "People. Process. AI. Outcomes." sits under the logo in the nav.
- Descriptor "Coordination tax, visible, measurable, reducible. For regulated processes." sits in the footer and at section 1's eyebrow.
- Unifize blue `#0052FF`. No purple.
- No em dashes anywhere in copy or comments.
- "Regulated processes" appears in the descriptor, in section 4, and in the footer.
- Sections all minimum 100vh.
- Linear-style sticky nav. Dark hero. Light sections after, alternating with papersoft and dark for emphasis.
