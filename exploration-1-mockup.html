EXPLORATION 1 — THE PILE-UP (with surface detail)

Systems of Record vs. Systems of Coordination · the cost of misalignment, made visible · near-iso, az 20°, el 30°

SYSTEMS OF RECORD

Graphite cluster · y_top = 0.6

6 systems labelled QMS · ERP · PLM · DMS · LIMS · MES.

Each has 4-square glyph + monospace label on top face.

SYSTEMS OF COORDINATION

Blue cluster · y_top = 0.2

Type-icons on each: chat bubble, envelope, calendar, doc-lines, grid, threads.

Daily-tool visual language.

THE PILE-UP

Cream paper-residue · y = 0

~50 fragments with ink: text-lines, grid, check/X, signature, stamp.

Each scrap reads as what it actually is.

Three places to look: (1) the labels and 4-square glyphs on the records — does this read as multiple systems rather than one platform? (2) the icons on the coordination cluster — do they feel like daily tools without overpowering the silhouette? (3) the pile-up — at hover-zoom, can you see that some scraps are emails, some are spreadsheet fragments, some are signed forms? The plinth elevation gap (Record 0.6, Coord 0.2) is the structural signal under the recognition layer.
