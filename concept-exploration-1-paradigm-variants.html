Concept Sketch Exploration 1 Paradigm Variants

Same Floor.
Two Unifize Geometries.

SoR / SoC stay identical across both. Coordination tax still rises inside SoC, atoms still hover, debris still piles between. The only thing that changes is what Unifize is and where it sits — neither version puts it in the gap between SoR and SoC.

PARADIGM 1

The Observatory

Unifize is a standalone tower at the front of the scene — separate from SoR and SoC, never between them. Its front facade is the audit-trail surface. Capture filaments arc across the floor to the tower's base.

PARADIGM 2

The Cutaway Excavation

The floor is cut away in front. Underneath: years of buried strata — color-coded fragments packed in horizontal layers. Unifize is the box that sits over the cut — it collects the buried records, then a conveyor belt extends out the front with clean audit-trail records riding on it.
